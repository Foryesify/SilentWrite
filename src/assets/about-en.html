About SilentWrite

There are already plenty of nice Markdown editors out there, like iA Writer, Ulysses, Nomo, and others. And I've tried quite a few of them.

But honestly, none of them really clicked with me. They all have their own flaws, and I just couldn't find a solution that felt truly comfortable.

Some are paid. Some have clunky interfaces. Some are paid and Mac-only. And a few are basically on par with VSCode—or even worse.

iA and Ulysses are two of the paid ones. Their design philosophies are decent, but they're also pretty pricey—clearly aiming for the premium market.

That said, I do have my reasons for not paying up. Ulysses isn't minimalist enough for my taste and locks you into the Apple ecosystem. iA is even more stubborn—it refuses to add interface animations or most customization options. Feels like going backward, honestly.

A few years ago, I stumbled upon Linho's blog while searching for something on Bing. He built a writing app called Clear Writer, modeled after iA Writer—clean interface, smooth experience, and an absolute joy to write in.

That inspired me to build my own Markdown editor—one that gives me a great experience.

At the end of the day, what writing really needs isn't a super powerful tool—it's a distraction-free environment.

That's why SilentWrite exists. That's what it's aiming for.

Why SilentWrite

I'm committed to building a writing app that:

  • Works across platforms with a solid web version, and feels great on mobile too
  • Supports PWA and plays nice with Safari, so it can serve as a solid alternative on iOS
  • Doesn't force you to pay
  • Keeps the interface clean and minimal—lets you pick your preferences during initial setup, then tweak them later in settings (with enough detail to satisfy even the most obsessive among us)
  • Offers backup functionality on the web version
  • Provides a clean, efficient file management system with support for folders, individual files, and password protection
  • Supports cloud sync
  • Pays obsessive attention to every little detail—cursor, scrollbar, selection highlights, animations, you name it
  • Supports transparent backgrounds
